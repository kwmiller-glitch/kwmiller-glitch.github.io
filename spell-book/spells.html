Spell Dictionary

Advanced Dungeons & Dragons, 2nd Edition: every spell in the Player’s Handbook

Press ☆ beside any spell to add it to My spells. The list is kept by this browser on this device; or to move it to another browser or computer.

The range, components, duration, casting time, area and saving throw are the Player’s Handbook’s printed values. Each description is a condensed summary, not the book’s text: unusual cases and material components are left out, so check the book for a close ruling. Cleric and druid filters follow the PHB’s major and minor spheres (minor spheres up to 3rd-level spells). Phantasmal Killer’s “+1/” modifier is a misprint in the book, shown as printed; Forbiddance is missing from the book’s list by sphere and is filed under Protection, as its own entry says.
